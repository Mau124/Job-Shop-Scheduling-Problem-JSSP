# Project JSP

**Title:** *Solving Job-Shop Scheduling via Search, Heuristics, Local Search, and CSP*  
**Instances:** You may use (i) your own generated instances and (ii) standard benchmark instances (e.g., OR-Library or equivalent).

---

## Goal

Implement and experimentally compare **search** and **constraint satisfaction** approaches for the classic **Job-Shop Scheduling Problem (JSP)** with the objective of **minimizing makespan**, under a rigorous and reproducible evaluation protocol.

---

## Problem Definition (Classic JSP)

- There are jobs and machines.
- Each job consists of operations in a fixed order.
- Each operation must be processed on a specific machine for a given duration.
- Constraints:
  1. **Precedence:** operation cannot start before finishes.
  2. **Machine capacity:** each machine processes at most one operation at a time (no overlap).
  3. **No preemption:** once started, an operation runs to completion.
- **Objective:** minimize makespan.

---

## Minimum Technical Requirements

**Must include all four.**

### 1. Uninformed Search (Baseline)

Acceptable examples:

- IDS/DFS with pruning/bounds
- UCS if your modeling supports it
- BFS on very small instances (with justification)

### 2. Heuristic Search

**A\*** or **Weighted A\*** (recommended), with at least **two heuristics**:

- **H1:** cheap (fast, less informative)
- **H2:** more informative (possibly more expensive)

### 3. Local Search

Use one of:

- Multi-start hill climbing
- Simulated annealing
- Tabu search

You must explicitly define your **representation** and **neighborhood**:

- At least 2 neighborhoods, **or**
- 1 neighborhood + a strong variant

### 4. CSP Approach

Backtracking with at least:

- **MRV**
- **LCV**

Forward checking is recommended.

> Libraries are allowed. However, you are still responsible for understanding, adapting, and defending the approach and all experimental results.

# Experimental Protocol (Fair Comparison)

- **Time budget:** maximum **60 seconds per instance per algorithm**.

- You must report:
  - Final makespan
  - Time to best solution
  - Success rate (when applicable)
  - When applicable:
    - Expanded nodes
    - Maximum frontier size

- You must include **ablation studies**:
  - **A\*:** H1 vs. H2 (and, if using Weighted A\*, at least two values of *w*)
  - **CSP:** MRV/LCV enabled vs. disabled (or vs. a naive ordering baseline)
  - **Local search:** neighborhood A vs. neighborhood B (or restarts vs. no restarts)
